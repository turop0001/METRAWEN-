Frequently asked questions

How do you clean your tools?

Every metal implement is cleaned, disinfected and sterilized in a medical-grade autoclave, then sealed in a pouch that we open in front of you. Files and buffers are single-use.

What products do you use?

CND and OPI for nails, Redken and Olaplex for color, and Dermalogica for facials. If you have allergies or sensitivities, let us know when you book.

What’s your cancellation policy?

Reschedule or cancel for free up to 24 hours before your appointment. Later cancellations and no-shows are charged 50% of the service. Just text or call us.

What if I’m running late?

We hold your spot for 15 minutes. After that we may need to shorten the service or move you to the next open slot so the next client isn’t kept waiting.

How long does a first visit take?

A gel manicure takes about an hour, color appointments 2 to 4 hours. Before any major color change we offer a free 15-minute consultation.
