Frequently asked questions

How do you handle data confidentiality?

We sign an NDA before any data changes hands. Access to your accounting system and bank feeds is read-only, and it's revoked once the engagement ends.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, calls happen over Zoom or phone, and data is shared through cloud spreadsheets. For clients in the New York City area, in-person meetings are available about once a month.

Will we need to switch accountants?

No. Your accountant or bookkeeper keeps handling tax filings, and I build management reporting on top of the same data, coordinating with them on the format of exports.

When will we see the first results?

You'll get your first management report with P&L and cash flow 3-4 weeks after we start. The impact on margin is usually visible after 2-3 months.

What if we track everything in spreadsheets, not accounting software?

That's normal for companies under $5M in revenue. We'll start with your spreadsheets, and I'll only suggest moving to a system like QuickBooks once it pays for itself.
